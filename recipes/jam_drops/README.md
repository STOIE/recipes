## Jam Drops

#### Ingredients

* 500 grams butter, softened
* 400 grams caster sugar
* 1 tablespoon vanilla essence
* 4 eggs
* 800 grams self-raising flour
* roughly 400 grams jam


#### Method

1. Preheat the oven to 180 deg C.
1. Line several baking trays with non-stick baking paper.
1. Place butter, sugar and vanilla in a mixing bowl and beat with a whisk until creamy. (stand mixer with whisk attachment)
1. Then, add the eggs and beat them in.
1. Sift in the flour, while mixing through with a spatula. (stand mixer with paddle attachment)
1. Place into rough golf ball sized blobs on the baking trays with a good 100-120mm spacing between each blob).
1. Place into the oven for a minute until the mixture softens a bit, then remove.
1. Using your finger or a spoon, make a small indent in the top of each blob (being sure not to make it too thin under the indent) and place s scoop of jam into it.
1. Return to the oven and bake until the edges have lightly browned.
1. Remove and let rest for 5 minutes in the tray, until you can remove and place onto a cooling rack.
