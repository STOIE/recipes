#### Ingredients

##### Main

* 1000 grams chicken thigh fillet, cut into medallions
* 1 teaspoon fine salt
* 1-1/2 tablespoons soy sauce
* 2 tablespoons Shaoxing wine (or dry sherry)
* 2 tablespoons cornflour
* 6 slices ginger, bruised
* 10 cloves garlic, peeled and roughly chopped
* 2 small brown onion, peeled and cut into 3cm chunks
* 1 large red bull horn chilli (else, capsicum), cut into 3cm chunks
* 1 large yellow bull horn chilli (else, capsicum), cut into 3cm chunks
* 1 large green bull horn chilli (else, capsicum), cut into 3cm chunks
* 1 cup unsalted roasted cashew nuts

##### Sauce

* 2 tablespoons oyster sauce
* 1-1/2 tablespoons soy sauce
* 1 tablespoon Shaoxing wine (or dry sherry)
* 1 tablespoon white vinegar
* 2 teaspoons sugar
* 1/2 cup chicken stock or water


#### Method

1. Velvet the chicken by combining it with 1 tablespoon of baking soda (bi-carb) and letting it rest for 60 minutes, then rinse the chicken.
1. Combine the sauce ingredients and set aside.
1. Add a bit of cold water to the cornstarch and make a slurry.
1. Combine the rinsed chicken with the soy sauce, salt and shaoxing wine mix well to coat the chicken.
1. Heat your wok and a decent amount of oil, start by frying the onion, tossing rapidly until lightly scorced, then remove and set aside.
1. Next, fry the bull horn chillis (or capsicums), same process, then remove and set aside.
1. Then ensure a decent little pool of oil is left in the bottom of the wok, else top it up and fry the chicken.
1. The chicken in this recipe is not meant to be scorched, so add some water after a minute or two to stop it browning too much.
1. Once cooked through, remove the chicken and set aside.
1. Now with a small bit of oil in the wok, fry the garlic and ginger quickly, do not burn it, once done, add the chicken back in.
1. Also add the vegetables back in and stir through, then add the slurry.
1. Mix the slurry through and let everything cook for a moment longer before adding the sauce.
1. With the sauce added, continue to mix through and then add the cashews.
1. Continue to toss everything together then heat off and remove for serving.
